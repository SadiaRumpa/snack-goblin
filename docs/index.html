📜 PRIVACY POLICY

Snack Goblin is designed with player privacy as our top priority. We comply with Apple App Store and Google Play privacy requirements:

• Personal Data: We do not collect names, email addresses, location, or private identifiers.

• Local Storage: High scores, coins, unlocked skins, and settings are stored locally on your device.

• Leaderboard: The optional Global Leaderboard stores only your chosen nickname and score in Google Firebase Firestore.

• Children's Privacy: Snack Goblin is family friendly and COPPA compliant.
